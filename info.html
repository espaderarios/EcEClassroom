Discover Classrio

Classrio helps students retain knowledge with collaborative flashcards while giving teachers the tools to organize lessons, track progress, and share resources securely.

Why Classrio?

Designed for blended classrooms, Classrio makes it effortless to build engaging study decks, synchronize content across devices, and keep learners motivated through interactive assessments.

Key Features

  • Dynamic flashcards with multimedia support
  • Teacher-managed classes and assignments
  • Community library for curated study sets
  • Secure publishing with owner controls

Ready to Explore?

Create your account, import existing decks, or start fresh with AI-assisted generation. Classrio is built to scale with your classroom.
